The project

A group data visualisation project for F1 fans, initially studying the 2026 Austrian Grand Prix. It explores race pace, tyre age and pit-stop context. The team workflow names me as Head Developer.

Technical approach

The preparation pipeline retains selected source laps and marks eligibility for normal-racing pace comparisons. A Streamlit story prototype combines pace context, matched race-window comparisons, stint accounting and pit/VSC exploration.

Design decisions

Keeping the recorded laps and their eligibility flags helps make the basis of comparisons visible. Race-window and stint context matter when interpreting differences in lap time.

Evidence

The repository includes the audience brief, data audit, preparation pipeline and story prototype, together with documentation of the comparisons and their limits.

Current status

Audience feedback and the final story remain in progress. Fuel load, traffic and conditions also affect lap times; the data cannot isolate causal tyre degradation or establish an optimal race strategy.
